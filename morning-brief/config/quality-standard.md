# Final Version Quality Standard

This project should behave like a private intelligence product, not a news digest.

## Editorial standard

- Lead with one clear daily thesis.
- Use the minimum number of story clusters needed to fully orient the reader.
- Synthesize overlapping coverage instead of listing articles one by one.
- Cover both finance-markets and world-geopolitics.
- Explicitly separate signal from noise.
- Include at least one idea that gives the reader a conversational or strategic edge.

## Source standard

- Use Tier 1 reporting for factual grounding whenever possible.
- Use Morning Brew-family newsletters for framing and prioritization, not as the sole authority.
- Prefer direct publisher links.
- Avoid duplicate stories unless a second source materially changes the interpretation.

## Writing standard

- Punchy and concise.
- Strong headlines.
- Short paragraphs.
- Every story answers `why it matters now`.
- Plain-English explanations over jargon.
- Mobile-first formatting.

## Cognitive-load standard

- No giant walls of text.
- Strong headings and visual separation.
- Short blocks that are easy to finish.
- Summary before depth.
- Consistent modules that reduce working-memory burden.

## Delivery standard

- One source of truth for briefing content.
- Generated outputs must be refreshed before email delivery.
- Send exactly one email per run.
- Do not send if the edition feels like a recap rather than an intelligence brief.
- Do not send if content still contains placeholders, malformed text, or obvious encoding issues.

## Safety standard

- Never store account credentials in the project.
- Never invent facts to fill space.
- If coverage is thin, be honest and send a lighter edition.
